Welcome to the product team's meeting notes.

Meeting background
The team is preparing the next release of the local document reader. This meeting covers the release plan, spending limits, and deployment dependencies.
The product supports offline use on personal computers and automated calls from an agent.

Key decisions
The team decided to use local RapidOCR NCNN with a maximum side of 960 and two OpenMP threads. No private Python environment is required.
The budget must not exceed 12345.67 yuan. Additional spending requires approval from project lead Alex Chen.
The deadline is November 6, 2026. A stable release is allowed only after testing on an actual Windows machine.

Risks and actions
Risk: tiny fonts and heavily blurred scans may lose characters. Critical amounts must be checked against the original image.
Morgan Lee will test OCR in both Windows packages before Friday.
Until actual Windows testing is complete, provide a release candidate and do not claim Windows execution has passed.

Office information
Coffee beans are restocked every Wednesday. Open the meeting room windows when the weather is clear.
The reading corner has three new novels. The borrowing register is at reception.
Lunch options include rice, noodles, and vegetables.
